## 3. Full Version

Dear [Name],

I am an independent researcher working on a project I call Field 01. The project is an attempt to develop an interpretational and eventually mathematical framework for describing particles, mass, memory, and black-hole horizons in terms of phase structure and recording.

I want to be explicit about the status of the work. I am not presenting Field 01 as a completed physical theory, nor as a replacement for the Standard Model, quantum field theory, or general relativity. At the present stage, it is a research program: a structured attempt to formulate a set of ideas carefully enough that they can be criticized, compared with established physics, and possibly formalized.

The current working idea is that an elementary particle may be interpreted as a stable closed phase configuration rather than as a point-like object, while a black-hole horizon may be interpreted as a boundary recording surface rather than as a literal point-singularity endpoint. In this language, a photon is treated as open phase transport, a massive particle as a closed configuration with a retained scalar/normal profile, and a horizon as a surface where volumetric phase structure is represented as boundary information.

I am aware that these ideas are speculative. For that reason, the current papers explicitly distinguish between:

1. established physics;
2. Field 01 interpretation;
3. new hypotheses;
4. open problems requiring formalization.

I would be grateful if you were willing to look at the material and provide any critical comments. The most useful feedback would be about where the framework is unclear, where it conflicts with known physics, which claims should be weakened, what literature should be added, and what would be required for a meaningful mathematical formulation.

The two main conceptual working papers are:

- `Elementary Particle as a Closed Wave`;
- `Horizon as a Phase Recording Surface`.

There is also a more technical supplement, `A Formalization Program for Field 01`, which I would send only if the mathematical structure is of interest.

There is also a project glossary and a roadmap. I would be grateful for even brief comments on whether this has any value as an interpretational framework or whether there are obvious issues I should address first.

Best regards,

[Name]

## 7. What To Ask For

Ask for:

- critical feedback;
- conceptual weak points;
- missing references;
- mathematical obstacles;
- conflicts with established theory;
- suggestions for formalization;
- whether the framework is worth developing as an interpretation.

Do not ask for:

- support for a new theory;
- endorsement;
- publication help immediately;
- confirmation that the model is correct.

## 10. Notes On The Hawking-Radiation Issue

This is the most sensitive part of the project. The safest wording is:

```text
Field 01 does not simply reject Hawking radiation. It asks whether the thermality associated with horizons can be interpreted as a reduced-access description of boundary phase information, rather than as literal loss of a horizon record.
```

Avoid saying:

```text
Hawking radiation is false.
```

Better:

```text
The project explores an alternative interpretation of the physical meaning of horizon thermality.
```
