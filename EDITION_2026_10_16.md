# Methodological Paper Edition — 16 October 2026

This is version 0.2 of **Reading a Past Label versus Updating a Surface Record: An Explicit Bulk--Boundary Example**, superseding the v0.1 edition dated 7 October 2026.

## Changes in v0.2

The manuscript now includes Section 4.2, “Comparison with established constructions,” and an expanded companion reference list. It places the boundary-oscillator model alongside George's field--oscillator system, brane--bulk string--oscillator analogues, endpoint-mass and dynamical Wentzell models, and the established state-space, lumpability, and predictive input--output frameworks of Frazzoli; Horstmeyer and Atay; Shalizi and Crutchfield; and Barnett and Crutchfield. The comparison explicitly limits the paper's contribution to its worked example and makes no priority claim for the model class, linear-response identities, or general predictive-sufficiency ideas.

The title page identifies the issue date and version. The equations and scope are unchanged: the paper does not test Field 01's cosmological proposal or claim autonomous or persistent physical memory.
